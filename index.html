Travel tip

When is the best time to visit France?

Late spring (May–June) and early fall (September–early October). The weather is mild, the days are long, and it's less crowded and cheaper than July and August.

  • 🗼Paris: April–June or September. Many small shops and restaurants close for a few weeks in August.
  • 🌊Côte d'Azur: June or September, when the sea is warm and the beaches are calmer.
